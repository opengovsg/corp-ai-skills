# Procurement appendix

Append to the end of the PRD only when flagged at the start of the interview (auto-recommended when the PRD will go to Corp team or vendor purchase approval).

---

```markdown
---

## SVP Justification
One paragraph. Why this spend is necessary and appropriate given org scale, risk, and operational needs. Frame around risk, scale, compliance, or efficiency. Not features.

## Cost Reasonableness
One paragraph. Why this vendor and cost are appropriate. What makes it uniquely suited vs alternatives. Tie back to Alternatives Considered.
```
