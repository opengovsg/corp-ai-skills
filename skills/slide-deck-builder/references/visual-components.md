# Visual components

Use this as a menu when the content needs a familiar composition. Select only the treatment that clarifies the current slide; a deck is not expected to demonstrate every pattern.

- **Type-led:** one large claim with restrained supporting copy. Use when the words are the visual.
- **Card group:** two to four genuine peer categories, each with a short token, title and one-line description.
- **Comparison:** two balanced sides for before/after, option A/B or old/new.
- **Stat callout:** one dominant number or percentage with a short interpretation.
- **Step flow:** a real sequence with concise numbered stages and directional movement.
- **Quote:** one short quotation with attribution when the speaker materially strengthens the claim.
- **Labelled list:** a small set of agenda items, features or checks with numbers or short tokens.
- **Code:** a readable monospace example on a quiet surface.
- **Timeline:** events or phases whose order and spacing matter.
- **Image and caption:** a screenshot, diagram or photograph that carries evidence or explanation.
- **Metric group:** three or four related measures whose comparison is the point.

Prefer a flat canvas over interface-like chrome. Adjacent slides should vary in silhouette when their narrative jobs differ.
Use numbers or short text tokens by default; use emoji only when the user asks for them.
